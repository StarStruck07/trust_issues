What we do

Trust Issues is a counterparty risk desk for logistics companies. Every week it checks the carriers, warehouses and customers you depend on, gives each one a sourced verdict, and drafts the next step for a person to approve.

  1. 01

    Check

    Identity, headcount, hiring, news and insolvency registries, through Crustdata.

  2. 02

    Judge

    Trusted, doubts, don't trust, or can't tell. Every fact cited and dated.

  3. 03

    Act

    Terms email, credit hold, backup quote. Drafted, approved by you, sent once.

We don't guess. If we can't tell who a company is, we say so. Every fact comes with a receipt: where it came from, which field, how old. A source that goes quiet is not good news. And nothing you can't take back leaves this building without a human saying yes.

rule 01

No receipt, no claim.

Every fact carries its source, the field it was read from and the record's date. Stale evidence is shown, never scored.
