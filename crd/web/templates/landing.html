Three
house rules

Trust issues are a feature. These are enforced in the code, not promised in a deck.

01

No receipt, no claim.

Every fact carries where it came from, which field, and how old the record is. Stale evidence is shown, never scored.

{{ sourced }} of {{ facts }} facts this week link to their source.
02

Silence isn't health.

A lookalike name, a missing domain or a source that times out never becomes a green light. We say can't tell and show what we tried.

You sign the scary stuff.
